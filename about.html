About SAARATHI

SAARATHI is a community-driven non-profit organization committed to improving access to education for children in government schools. We work hand-in-hand with students, teachers, and parents to reduce learning gaps and provide meaningful support. Through educational and social initiatives, we strive to empower communities and ensure that every child has the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive.

Empowering Education, Inspiring Confidence

At SAARATHI, we believe every child deserves the chance to learn, grow, and dream—regardless of their background. Working closely with government schools across Karnataka, we support students from economically disadvantaged communities by bridging learning gaps and nurturing confidence in their educational journey.

Who We Are

SAARATHI works closely with government schools across Karnataka to support students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. Through school visits, student engagement activities and educational resource distribution, the organization strives to reduce learning gaps and encourage children to pursue their education with confidence.

Our volunteers interact with teachers, parents and students to understand their needs and provide meaningful support such as school bags, notebooks, stationery kits, and learning resources that help improve the overall educational environment.

What We Do :

• School Engagement: Regular visits to understand the needs of students, teachers, and parents. • Resource Distribution: Providing school bags, notebooks, stationery kits, and learning materials to create a supportive environment. • Student Activities: Interactive sessions that encourage curiosity, teamwork, and self-belief. • Community Support: Collaborating with teachers and parents to strengthen the ecosystem around each child.

Our Impact

• Reduced barriers to learning by ensuring children have the essentials. • Boosted classroom participation and confidence among students. • Strengthened collaboration between schools, families, and volunteers. • Inspired children to pursue education with renewed enthusiasm.
